## 1. About Me

I am a postgraduate student in the Master of Cyber Security programme at Universiti Malaya, building on a Bachelor of Computer Science (Cybersecurity major) from the University of Queensland and a Bachelor of Informatics from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) in Indonesia. My background combines backend web development with network security analysis. I have built a cryptography platform using RSA, AES and digital signatures, simulated a firewall and intrusion detection system in Python, and completed a network service innovation internship at Telkomsel, where I helped analyse the security of company products and services. I am drawn to cybersecurity because it rewards curiosity and careful analysis, and because it turns complex technical findings into clear decisions that protect real people. My goal is to contribute to research in computer system and security.
